Rules the design enforces

Posts only come from completed sessions — the composer is the completion flow.
One shared post per session, on every participant's profile. Organizer edits/deletes; anyone can remove themselves.
Join only via invite, direct add, or a valid link — never from the feed.
A streak week counts when two friends complete ≥1 session together, Mon–Sun.
