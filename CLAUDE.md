# CLAUDE.md

## Design system

This app follows the **Mat Monkey design system**. For any UI, styling, layout, copy-in-UI, prototype or brand-asset work (SwiftUI or web):

1. Use the `mat-monkeys-design` skill (`.claude/skills/mat-monkeys-design/SKILL.md`) and follow its styling rules and guardrails.
2. Treat `DESIGN.md` as the source of truth, and take exact values from `.claude/design-system-spec.json`. Don't invent colors, fonts, radii or spacing that aren't in the spec.
