Activity · This week · design exploration

Tap a day, it's logged.

Both directions drop the “What did you hit today?” sheet. One tap counts the day straight away. Techniques become an optional follow-up below the calendar, and whatever you count still feeds the technique charts in Stats. Both phones open just after tapping Friday. Tap any past day, or Replay the tap, to see it again.

Direction A

Tally on the card

The week card opens a small speech bubble under the day you tapped. Your techniques sit there as chips: tap one to count a hit. Nothing leaves the card.

How it behaves

  • Tap a day: it fills coral with a bounce, and the bubble points at it with “Friday logged”.
  • Tap a chip to add one hit. A counted chip turns paper-white with a coral count and a − to take one back.
  • Hits save as you tap (“Saved to Stats”). Undo in the bubble removes the log and its hits.
  • Close it or tap another day and it folds away. Ignoring it is fine; the day is already counted.
Strong atFastest possible: one tap per hit, no modes or steppers. Feels like part of the calendar.
Watch forGrows the dark card. Chip rows get busy past about 8 techniques; we'd show recents and keep “+ Move” for the rest.
Direction B

Pull-out tray

A white tray slides out from under the week card: “Friday's logged”, with one quiet “Add hits” button. Opening it gives the familiar counters in place, and closing it leaves a summary.

How it behaves

  • Tap a day: it fills coral, a “Training logged · Undo” toast confirms it, and the tray slides out.
  • The tray asks once, softly. “Add hits” opens the − / + counters you already know, right in the tray.
  • Counts save as you go. Done collapses the tray to a summary such as “Heel hook ×2”, with Edit.
  • × or “Not now” tucks the tray away. The calendar card itself never changes size.
Strong atCalm and clearly optional. Keeps the dark card as it is, and the counters scale to long lists.
Watch forOne extra tap (Add hits) before counting. Pushes Coming up further down while it's open.

  • Planned days keep the chooser. Switch to “Planned session today” and tap Friday. The sheet still offers the planned session and a quick log. The quick log option now logs immediately and closes the sheet, then the same optional technique prompt appears. Choosing the plan still goes to Complete & share.
  • Stats stay honest. Every tap creates a quick log. Hits you count attach to that log, so the existing technique charts in Stats pick them up unchanged. A tap with no hits counts the day and adds nothing to the charts.
