Activity · This week · design exploration

Tap a day, it's logged.

Round 3 adds a quieter option, C. In all three, tapping a day no longer opens the “What did you hit today?” sheet: one tap counts the day straight away. Below the days, an optional follow-up lets you count techniques or tap Not now, and whatever you count still feeds the technique charts in Stats. Every phone opens just after tapping Friday. Tap any past day, or Replay the tap, to see it again.

Direction A

Moves row

The week card grows a second row under the days. Your techniques use the same circles as the days, so counting a move works just like logging a day: tap it and it fills coral.

How it behaves

  • Tap a day: it fills coral, a “Training logged · Undo” toast confirms it, and a row of technique circles opens under the days: “What landed on Friday?”
  • Tap a circle to count one hit. It fills coral and shows the count. The small − on it takes one back, like the ×N badge on a day.
  • Hits save as you tap. “Not now” folds the row away, and it turns into “Done” once you've counted something.
  • More techniques scroll sideways. The last circle adds a new move.
Strong atOne tap per hit. It reads as part of the calendar: same circles, same coral, no new surface.
Watch forThe dark card gets taller while it's open. Past five techniques you scroll sideways to find one.
Direction B

Tap pad

A full-width white card lands just below the calendar, with its own space. Each technique is a large tile with its count, and you tap a tile to count a hit. Not now clears it away.

How it behaves

  • Tap a day: it fills coral, the toast confirms it, and the tap pad drops in under the week card: “Friday's logged. Anything land?”
  • Tap anywhere on a tile to count one hit. Counted tiles turn coral-tinted, with a − button in the corner.
  • Hits save as you tap. “Not now” becomes “Done” once you've counted something. Either one clears the card.
  • The calendar card never changes. The dashed tile adds a new move.
Strong atEasy to see and easy to hit: large targets and counts you can read at a glance. Clearly skippable.
Watch forIt adds a card above Coming up while it's open. Past six techniques the grid gets tall.
Direction C · new

Quiet line

The subtle one. Logging a day adds a single line at the bottom of the week card: “Friday logged”, a small Add moves pill and Not now. No techniques are listed and nothing opens until you ask.

How it behaves

  • Tap a day: it fills coral, the toast confirms it, and one line fades in under the days. The Add moves pill gives two soft coral pulses, then stays still.
  • Add moves is the only way in. It opens a short sheet, “What landed on Friday?”, with the − / + counters for subs, sweeps and passes.
  • Counts save as you tap. After Done, the line shows what you added (“Heel hook ×2 · Armbar”) with Edit.
  • Not now removes the line. The card goes back to exactly how it looks today.
Strong atBarely changes the screen. Logging stays one tap, and the invitation is easy to ignore.
Watch forEasy to miss for new users. Counting takes two taps (Add moves, then +), and it uses a sheet again, though only on request.

Same in all three

  • Planned days keep the chooser. Switch to “Planned session today” and tap Friday. The sheet still offers the planned session and a quick log. The quick log option now logs immediately and closes the sheet, then the same optional technique follow-up appears. Choosing the plan still goes to Complete & share.
  • Stats stay honest. Every tap creates a quick log. Hits you count attach to that log, so the existing technique charts in Stats pick them up unchanged. A tap with no hits counts the day and adds nothing to the charts.
  • Not now is always one tap away. The follow-up never blocks the log. Not now (or Done after counting) clears it, and the day stays counted.
  • Removing works as today. The ×N badge on a day still removes the latest quick log, along with any hits on it.
